The Best At-Home IPL, LED, and Laser Hair Removal Devices, Compared

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This tool compares the most popular at-home IPL, LED, and laser hair removal devices based on the specs that actually matter to users: price range, skin/hair compatibility, performance (fluence), treatment frequency, and comfort-enhancing features. All data is sourced from manufacturer specifications.

IPL vs. LED vs. laser: IPL devices are a cost-effective option that deliver results for candidates with pale skin and dark hair. Laser and LED devices are significantly more powerful, which makes them more effective for treating stubborn areas like the bikini region or male facial hair. They are also safer for those with dark skin tones due to the wavelengths they emit.
